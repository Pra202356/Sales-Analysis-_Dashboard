# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance across different regions, product categories, customer segments, and time periods. The dashboard provides a clear overview of key business metrics and helps identify sales trends, regional performance, product performance, and customer purchasing patterns.

## 📌 Project Overview

The Sales Analysis Dashboard was developed to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights using Power BI.

The dashboard enables users to monitor overall sales and profitability, analyze sales across different regions and states, compare customer segments, evaluate product categories, and identify top-performing products.

Interactive filters allow users to explore the data dynamically by Region and Year, making the dashboard useful for business performance analysis and decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

This dashboard was built using the following technologies:

- 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Developed the interactive dashboard and visualizations.
- 🔄 **Power Query** – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
- 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Created calculated measures and KPIs.
- 📈 **Data Visualization** – Designed KPI cards, donut charts, line charts, bar charts, maps, and slicers.
- 🗺️ **Map Visualization** – Visualized sales and profit performance across different states.
- 🎛️ **Interactive Filtering** – Implemented Region and Year slicers for dynamic analysis.
- 📁 **File Formats**
  - `.pbix` – Power BI dashboard file
  - `.png` – Dashboard preview image


## ✨ Dashboard Features

### Business Problem

Businesses generate large amounts of sales data, making it difficult to quickly identify important trends and understand which regions, products, and customer segments contribute most to overall performance.

### Goal of the Dashboard

The dashboard was created to:

- Monitor overall sales and profitability
- Analyze sales performance across regions
- Track monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Compare customer segments
- Analyze product category performance
- Identify top-selling products
- Understand customer payment preferences
- Analyze sales and profit across different states

## 📊 Dashboard Walkthrough

### KPI Cards

The dashboard displays key business metrics including:

- **Total Sales**
- **Total Profit**
- **Average Order Value**
- **Total Orders**

### 📈 Sales by Month

A line chart displays monthly sales trends and allows comparison across different years. It helps identify changes in sales performance and monthly patterns.

### 🌍 Sales and Profit by State

An interactive map visualizes sales and profit across different states, helping identify geographical areas contributing to business performance.

### 👥 Sales by Segment

A donut chart shows the contribution of different customer segments:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

### 🌎 Sales by Region

Displays the percentage contribution of sales from:

- Central
- East
- South
- West

### 🛍️ Sales by Category

A bar chart compares sales performance across major product categories:

- Office Supplies
- Technology
- Furniture

### 📅 Sales by Quarter

A column chart compares sales across:

- Qtr 1
- Qtr 2
- Qtr 3
- Qtr 4

This helps identify quarterly sales patterns and high-performing periods.

### 🏆 Top 5 Products by Sales

Displays the five highest-performing products based on total sales, making it easy to identify products contributing strongly to revenue.

### 💳 Sales by Payment Mode

Shows how sales are distributed across different payment methods, including:

- Cash on Delivery (COD)
- Online
- Cards

### 🎛️ Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically filter the entire dashboard by:

- **Region**
- **Year**

The visuals and KPI cards automatically update according to the selected filters.

## 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard helps identify:

- Regions contributing the most to overall sales
- Customer segments generating higher sales
- Monthly and quarterly sales patterns
- Best-performing product categories
- Top-selling individual products
- Geographic distribution of sales and profit
- Customer payment preferences
